Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how it is used.

Last updated: September 2026

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry through this website, we collect the details you provide in the form — such as your name, phone number, email address, business/organisation name, the service you are enquiring about and your message.

How we use this information

We use the information you submit only to respond to your enquiry and to understand your accounting, tax or filing requirements. We do not sell or rent your information to any third party.

Third-party form processing

Enquiries submitted through our contact form are processed using a third-party form processing service, which delivers your submission to us by email. This service may process your data solely for the purpose of transmitting your enquiry and is not used by us for any other purpose.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the site and to help us improve it. These cookies do not identify you personally. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

External links

This website may link to external websites, including official government portals. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.
