FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
What services does Ajay Anand & Associates provide?
We provide accounting and bookkeeping, GST, income tax, TDS, audit and review support, payroll-related compliance, business registrations and tax notice assistance.
Do you provide monthly accounting services?
Yes. Monthly bookkeeping and accounting support can be provided based on the size and requirements of the business.
Do you handle GST return filing?
Yes. We provide GST compliance support including GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, annual return support, reconciliation and other applicable GST services.
Can you help with GST notices?
Yes. We can review the notice, analyse the relevant records and assist with preparing the required response and supporting documentation.
Do you provide Income Tax Return filing?
Yes. We provide ITR filing and related tax computation and compliance support for eligible individuals, professionals and businesses.
Do you provide TDS filing services?
Yes. We assist with TDS calculation, payment compliance, return filing, reconciliation and related notices.
Can you handle accounting remotely?
Yes. Depending on the nature of the engagement, accounting and compliance services can be provided using digital document-sharing and communication systems.
Do you provide services to businesses outside Bengaluru?
Yes. Many accounting and compliance activities can be handled digitally, subject to the nature of the engagement and applicable requirements.
How are professional fees decided?
Fees depend on factors such as the nature of services, transaction volume, number of GST registrations, complexity of accounts and frequency of compliance. We provide a clear fee proposal after understanding the client's requirements.
What documents are required to start accounting services?
The documents depend on the nature of the business and services required. Typically, we may require accounting records, bank statements, sales and purchase invoices, GST information and previous financial/tax records.
